Why Your Morning Routine Matters

Niacinamide vs Retinol: Which One is Right for You?

By Aisha Fernandez · April 28, 2025 · 8 min read

What is Niacinamide?

Niacinamide (Vitamin B3) is a gentle, multi-tasking powerhouse. It minimises pores, fades dark spots, controls oil production, and strengthens the skin barrier — all with minimal irritation.

What is Retinol?

Retinol is a Vitamin A derivative renowned for tackling fine lines, acne, and uneven texture. It speeds up cell turnover, but requires a slow introduction to avoid redness and peeling.

Can You Use Both?

Yes — but not at the same time. Use niacinamide in the morning and retinol only at night. Together over time, they complement each other beautifully for a refined, even complexion.
